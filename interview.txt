2. 工作中的挑战
•What is the biggest challenge you have faced at work?
•Tell me about a difficult situation you faced in your job and how you handled it.
•Describe a time when you faced a major obstacle at work.
--Answer---------------------------------------------
The biggest challenge I faced was leading the team to build a shared internal admin system.
and try to unified the user experience across all the internal system.

Each team in my previous company was building their own internal admin systems separately,
without a shared standard. As a result, the user experience was inconsistent and confusing.

The main challenge I faced was:
First, I needed to design a solution that different teams could adopt,
and convince each team to migrate their project to the unified user experience.

So here’s what I did:

First, I took the time to talk to people from ops and product.
I tried to find out what everyone really needed in common.

And then I designed the solution based on these needs.
The goal was simple:
For new projects, make it easy to use with just one click and immediately focus on business development.
For old projects, I built reusable components and published them to our internal NPM.
So old projects could install these packages and gradually update their UI.

Second, I create a clear migration guide 
and showed each team how easy it was to migrate their project.
So in the end, there was really no excuse not to cooperate.

After a few updates, we were able to unify the user experience.
Looking back, I think this was my biggest challenge so far.
It required not only technical work, but also a lot of communication.
And I’m really proud of the result.
