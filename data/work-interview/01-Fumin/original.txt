This system was to let users earn points by buying wealth products and then use them in the points mall.
The core goal was to encourage user behavior and create a positive cycle.
We built a mobile web points mall in the bank app where users could:
  browse products
  check and use points
  view orders and history
We also built an admin system to:
  manage merchants and products
  set redemption rules
  view statistics and reports


My responsibilities included leading the team to develop this system.
I worked with the product manager and backend team to clarify requirements,
reviewed code, and managed delivery progress and quality.


Increased daily UV by 30% and boosted wealth product purchases by 15%,
creating a positive user loyalty cycle.
