## Business Overview
The Technology Platforms Division (TPD) is responsible for building and operating the infrastructure and ecosystem platforms which power the Rakuten Group. Our mission is to provide our Rakuten Cloud and Ecosystem Platforms which will deliver Core Value to accelerate the growth of Rakuten Group services.

技术平台部门（TPD）负责构建和运营支撑乐天集团的基础设施与生态系统平台。我们的使命是提供乐天云与生态系统平台，创造核心价值，助力乐天集团各项服务的加速发展。

### Department Overview
The Incentive Platform Department (INPD) provides incentive[激励] and payment solutions that empower Rakuten's businesses around the world. The platforms are continually adding capabilities and scale to accelerate the Rakuten ecosystem synergies[协同].

Incentive Platform Department (INPD) 提供激励和支付解决方案，帮助乐天在全球范围内的业务发展。这些平台不断增强功能和扩大规模，以加速乐天生态系统内部的协同效应。
