1. 描述背景，存在的问题
When I was at my previous company, I led the effort to build a shared internal admin system template.
At the time, each team in the company was building their own internal admin systems separately without a shared standard.
As a result, the user experience was inconsistent and confusing.
The ops team often pointed out that the inconsistent interaction patterns across systems.

3. 开发系统，明确目标
Then I led the design and development of a shared internal admin system template.
The goal was simple:
For new project, I built a front-end template that works out of the box.
make it easy to use with just one click and immediately focus on business development,
For old project, I wrapped components like layout and table based on Ant Design and
I published them in the internal npm.
So old projects can install these packages gradually to update their UI.
After a few updates, we were able to unify the user experience across all systems.
