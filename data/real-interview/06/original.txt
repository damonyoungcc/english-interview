When I have multiple deadlines,
I first check which tasks are more urgent and more important.
I focus on high-impact and time-sensitive tasks first, then plan the rest.

For example, if I need to fix a bug, add a feature, and refactor some code,
I’ll fix the bug first because it affects users directly.
Then I work on the feature based on its deadline and priority.
I always talk to my team to adjust the plan and make sure we’re on the same page.


I always try to manage my time well and keep clear communication.
This always helps me to deliver project smoothly.
