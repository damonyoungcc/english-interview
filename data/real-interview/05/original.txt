At the beginning of my career, I was full of eager to contribute.
I took on a feature task but didn’t estimate the time properly.
I thought I could finish it quickly, 
but it turned out to be more complicated than I expected.

As a result, I didn’t complete it on time,
and the delivery was delayed.My team had to adjust the plan.

After that, First, I learned to break tasks into smaller steps, 
and Then plan by priority,
and always communicate with my team to make sure we’re on the same page.
It was a good lesson for me, and since then it never happened again.
