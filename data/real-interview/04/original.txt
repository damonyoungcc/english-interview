I think leadership is not about giving orders.
It’s about taking responsibility and leading by example.

In my previous company, we had an H5 page with a photo upload feature.
When users uploaded large images, it was slow.
Everyone knew about the issue, but no one wanted to work on it.

I decided to take the lead.
I did some research and used a front-end image compression technique.
It made the upload much faster and the experience smoother.

After that, I shared the solution with the team.
I also helped turn the upload logic into a reusable component with unit tests.

We promoted it across multiple projects in the company.
It significantly improved user experience.

This experience showed me that leadership is about action.
When you step up and solve real problems, others will follow.
