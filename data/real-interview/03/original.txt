In my previous company, I worked on improving the internal admin systems.

Different departments had different systems, and the user experience wasn’t consistent.

So I had two goals.
First, I wanted to understand everyone’s needs and create a standard user experience.
Second, I wanted to make the system simple and easy to migrate, so other teams would be willing to adopt it.

I started by learning about each team’s background and how they used their tools.
Then I explained my ideas using language they were familiar with — not just tech terms.

I also created a clear migration guide to make the switch easier.
It showed how the new design could improve their workflow and user experience.

During the whole process, I kept everyone updated and made sure we were on the same page.

After a few rounds of feedback and iteration, we successfully unified the UX across all teams.

New projects were delivered twice as fast, and users gave great feedback.
They said the new system was simple, clean, and easy to use.
