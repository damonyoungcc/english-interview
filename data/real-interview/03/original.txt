I try to unfiy the UX of our internal admin systems across teams.
To do that, I had to talk with people from different teams
and try to found out everyone really need in common.
and then convince each team to migrate to the unified UX

I think the first  point is to understanding their background
So I can ask them question using language they were familiar with
not too technical, but easy to understand.

Then The second point I also try to see things from their side,
I know each team had their own priorities, 
so the biggest thing I needed to do was make the migration as easy as possible.
I also provide a clear migration guide
to show how the new design could improve their workflow and easy to migrate.

So I believe good cross-team communication is about empathy, and being easy to work with.
