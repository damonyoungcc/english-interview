是我在上一家公司主动积极推动的一套后台管理系统模板的建设。
我们公司当时的情况是——因为各个部门都是独立开发自己的后台系统，缺乏统一规范。
这导致交互体验非常不一致。
尤其是运营的同事，经常反馈说不同系统的操作方式完全不一样。
切换起来很痛苦，效率也受到影响。
我在日常合作中发现这些问题越来越多，就主动花时间去了解大家的痛点。
我做了一些调研，也和运营、产品、开发等同事都聊了聊，总结出了一些高频的抱怨点，比如导航逻辑混乱、表格操作不一致、权限控制很繁琐等等。
所以我试着找出大家真正共同需要的功能，然后我主导设计并开发了一套通用的后台管理系统。
它的核心目标有两个：一是新项目能一键接入，拿来即用，立刻专注业务开发；二是老项目也可以低成本改造，逐步统一体验。
一开始，有些团队并不是很愿意迁移，因为他们有自己优先处理的事情和现有的定制系统。
我做了一个演示，写了文档，还提供了迁移指南，尽量让这个过程变得尽可能简单——所以最后，真的没有理由不配合了。
后来陆续有多个团队采用了这个模板，效果非常不错。

3. 纯英文版：

When I joined my previous company, I proactively led the effort to build a shared internal admin system template.
At the time, each team in the company was building their own internal admin systems separately without a shared standard.
As a result, the user experience was really inconsistent and confusing.
The ops team often pointed out that the inconsistent interaction patterns across systems
made it frustrating to use and slowed them down a lot.
I started noticing more and more issues during day-to-day collaboration, so I proactively took the time to understand everyone’s pain points.
I talked to people from ops, product, and dev teams, and found that a lot of them had the same complaints — like the navigation was confusing, tables worked differently in each system, and setting permissions was a pain.
So I tried to find what everyone really needed in common. Then I led the design and development of a shared internal admin system template.
The goal was simple: make it super easy for new projects to use with one click and immediately focus on business development, and help old ones upgrade without too much effort.
One thing I will say though, is that some teams weren’t that willing to migrate at first—they had their own priorities and custom setups.
I created a demo, wrote documentation, and provided a migration guide to make the process as easy as possible—so in the end, there was really no excuse not to cooperate.
Over time, more and more teams started using the template, and it worked really well. I’m really proud of that work.
