One time, I needed to unify the user experience of our internal admin systems across teams.
At my previous company, each team was building their own internal system separately, 
so the user experience was inconsistent and confusing for the ops team.

My task was to design a unified solution that different teams could adopt
and then convince each team to migrate their projects to this new standard.

First, I had a meeting with people from different teams to explain the goal.
Then I spent time discussing with each team and tried to find out what everyone needed in common.
Then, I summarized these needs into a clear list and designed a solution based on these needs.
I also thought from their perspective – I knew they always had their own priorities,
so I prepared clear migration guidelines and documentation
and showed each team how easy it was to migrate their projects.
By doing this, there was really no excuse not to cooperate.

After a few updates, we were able to unify the user experience.
It showed me how powerful clear communication and practical solutions can be.
