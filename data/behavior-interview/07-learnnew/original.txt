⭐ Situation
In my personal learning, I wanted to build a tool to help me study Japanese more effectively.

🎯 Task
The goal was to create a tool that could play audio and highlight text in sync.
To do this, I needed to generate a timestamped JSON file from the audio.

⚒️ Action
I handled this task in three steps.
First, I defined the goal clearly based on my learning needs.
Then, I researched the technology and read the documentation.
I found that Whisper, an AI speech-to-text model,
could help with generating accurate timestamps.
Finally, I wrote a Python script to process the audio
and built a simple UI to play the audio with synchronized text highlights. 
After testing, I launched it for my daily use.

✅ Result
Now I use this tool every day.
It helped me learn faster,
and showed me I can handle new challenges with confidence.
