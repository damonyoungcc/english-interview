## **Position Details**

**职位详情**

We are looking for an experienced frontend engineer who has a passion for working on complex/large systems and understands the importance of maintaining and supporting one as you will be working on developing user-facing web applications and components to support continuous improvement and growth our systems.
我们正在寻找一名经验丰富的前端工程师，对复杂和大型系统充满热情，并理解维护和支持这些系统的重要性，因为你将负责开发面向用户的网页应用程序和组件，以支持系统的持续改进与发展。

You will be joining one of our teams of diversely skilled engineers who actively support the maintenance of existing Rakuten system related to lifestyle and leisure related services.
你将加入由多技能工程师组成的团队，积极支持现有与生活方式及休闲服务相关的乐天系统的维护工作。

As part of our team, you will have the opportunity to
作为团队的一员，你将有机会：

* Design & Develop frontend features on small to large scale systems
  在小型到大型系统中设计与开发前端功能
* Learn about robust, secure, modular and maintainable code of frontend.
  学习编写健壮、安全、模块化且易于维护的前端代码
* Learn about and perform load/performance tests on high access systems
  学习并执行高访问量系统的负载与性能测试
* Interact and work with members from other development teams, Product Managers, UX/UI designers and QA engineers
  与其他开发团队、产品经理、UX/UI设计师以及QA工程师进行沟通与合作

A commitment to team collaboration, good communication and delivering quality product is important.
对团队协作、良好沟通以及交付高质量产品的承诺至关重要。
